Why this changes

Yegor pushed back on 12 Nov, and he is right. What was built is a CRM: every rep, every inbox, every chat, one timeline per customer. That is more personal data than marketing needs, and it still does not answer the question he expected: which content brings customers who buy.

The new aim, in Henry's and Yegor's words:

  • Social media → sales: when someone comes from a YouTube Short, a long video, Instagram, a comment or a DM and contacts us or buys, we see it. This is our biggest blind spot today.
  • What customers talk about: questions before buying, pain points, issues, products, what makes people hesitate, as summaries for marketing.
  • Both ways: sales sees which content actually sells, so they can send it.

The rule that follows: CTRL keeps topics and totals, not people's messages. No customer names on the page. Email and WhatsApp text is read once by the AI for its questions and topics, then thrown away.

How social media → sales is tracked

1. One link system, no raw links anywhere

Every link anyone at OneMiners posts becomes a short tracked link from CTRL, on our own address (for example go.oneminers.com/z15-yt). Each one carries a code that says exactly where it was posted: the platform, the post, and whether it sat in a bio, a description, a comment reply or a DM. CTRL counts every click, then sends the person on to where they wanted to go.

The team rule: never paste a raw website link or a raw WhatsApp number again. CTRL adds the tracked link by itself to every post it prepares or publishes. For comments and DMs, the team gets a short list of ready reply links per platform (Instagram DM, YouTube comment, TikTok DM and so on), one tap to copy.

2. Where the links go, platform by platform

Platform link rules change often; these were checked on 12 Nov 2026.

Shopify checkout The address they landed on The Shopify order

4. Joining it up

CTRL follows each code to the conversation it started, then matches that customer's email or phone across Sales Inquiries, Support Requests, Orders, the three inboxes and the support WhatsApp. The result is one path per customer: which post → which conversation → which ticket or inquiry → order or not. The page shows only the totals of those paths, never the people.

Honest limits: counting starts on the day the links go live, never backwards. A customer can delete the code from the typed WhatsApp message, or type our address by hand; then the "where did you find us?" answer is the fallback, and the page shows the share of unknown source openly instead of hiding it. Shorts and TikTok captions can't hold links, so those are tracked per channel, not per video, unless the customer names the video.

About the 143 number

The support number was suspended, never banned, on 5 Nov. It was signed in directly on a new phone instead of being paired, which kicked the other phone out; WhatsApp read the quick swap as possible theft. It has had no problems before or since.

For the Chatwoot connection this matters: connecting the number unlinks every paired device. They must be re-linked the normal way, by pairing (Linked devices → Link a device), never by signing in to the number on another phone. One phone stays the main phone throughout.
